# Table redesign

A new look for the game table: a round wooden table seen from above, name panels round it,
opponents' cards standing in racks on the rim, and panels pinned to the window corners.

- **Start here:** [`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md), the step-by-step plan for
  building it in the Qt client.
- **The target:** [`mockup/romanian_whist_table.html`](mockup/romanian_whist_table.html). Download it
  and open it in a browser; it is playable and works offline.
- **Screenshots** of the mock-up at 1920×1080: [`screenshots/`](screenshots/).
- **How it went, phase by phase:** [`history/index.html`](history/index.html). Open it in a
  browser: a slideshow of the client at every phase (the table, bidding and your hand), a wipe
  between 1.0 and 2.0, and a film of 3 min 12 s, each picture shown alone before its caption types out, [`history/redesign.mp4`](history/redesign.mp4).

Status: built. Phases 0 to 12 are complete in the client, and phase 13 holds the owner's final
changes; each phase's outcome, and the owner's decisions along the way, are in the plan. The
Romanian translation is reviewed. The client's headings are Belleza where the mock-up's are
Marcellus, which has no ș or ț, and the logo on the table is round.
